About the project

ExamShield AI

An offline-first exam proctoring system that combines camera analysis, incident logging, risk scoring, and reviewable reports.

What it does

ExamShield watches the exam camera for face presence, head orientation, multiple people, and mobile phones. Incidents are recorded once per discrete event and summarized when the session ends.

Technology stack

  • Python and Flask
  • OpenCV and MediaPipe
  • Ultralytics YOLO
  • SQLite and pandas
  • fpdf2 for PDF reports
